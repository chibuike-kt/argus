# Approval checklist

- Source: ARGUS Master ADR, section 19
- Version: 0.1.0-draft
- Last updated: 26 October 2026

---

The master ADR should not be marked approved until the owner has reviewed the unresolved items and accepted the material trade-offs.

- Confirm the consolidated product and execution boundaries.

- Approve or revise the modular backend and deployment architecture.

- Approve the market-data contract and retention assumptions.

- Review evidence for commercial market-data providers.

- Approve the charting solution after licensing and feature validation.

- Approve the initial strategy specification and numeric defaults.

- Approve lifecycle transition and event-precedence rules.

- Approve risk, entry, stop and target construction.

- Approve security and privacy implementation details.

- Approve recovery objectives against a costed operational plan.

- Approve the testing and shadow-promotion gates.

- Approve the implementation sequence.

- Resolve or explicitly accept the remaining open decisions.
